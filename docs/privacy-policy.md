# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** February 7, 2026

This application is a personal SMS coaching tool operated by a single individual for their own use.

## Data Collected

- Phone number (for sending/receiving SMS)
- Message content (to provide coaching responses)
- Training and nutrition data shared via text (stored to maintain coaching context)

## How Data Is Used

All data is used solely to provide personalized muscle growth coaching via SMS. Data is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third parties.

## Data Storage

Data is stored in Google Cloud Platform infrastructure. Conversation history is retained to maintain coaching context and may be deleted at any time by the user.

## Third-Party Services

- **Twilio** — SMS delivery ([Twilio Privacy Policy](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/privacy))
- **Anthropic** — AI coaching responses ([Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy))
- **Google Cloud** — Infrastructure and storage

## Contact

This is a personal project. For questions, open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/edward-cates/gmail-ai).

## Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting.
